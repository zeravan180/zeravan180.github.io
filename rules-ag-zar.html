🃏 AG Zar (dice and cards)

Use the dice to place all six of your cards in the middle first.

About the game

AG Zar is a light game made for AG Classic Games that mixes dice and cards. It is quick to learn and every roll gives a small decision, so it is good for short breaks.

How to play

  • Each player has six cards numbered 1 to 6.
  • On your turn, roll the die. If you roll a 3, your card 3 goes to the middle of the table.
  • You can also combine cards: with a 6 you may place the 6, or 5 and 1, or 4 and 2, or 3, 2 and 1 together.
  • If your opponent already has a card on that spot in the middle, it goes back to their hand and yours takes its place.
  • If you have no possible move, the turn passes to the next player.
  • The first player to get all six cards into the middle wins.

Tips

  • Placing several small cards with one big roll can be faster than placing one card.
  • Sometimes it is worth sending your opponent's card back instead of placing a new one.
  • Keep the cards that are hard to place (like 6) for when a big roll comes.

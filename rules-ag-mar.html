🪱 AG Mar (worm game)

Eat, grow and trap other worms in a fast arcade game.

About the game

AG Mar is an arcade game made for AG Classic Games. You steer a worm across a large arena full of glowing food. Every piece of food makes the worm longer, and the aim is to become the longest worm on the leaderboard.

How to play

  • Use the joystick, or move your finger on the screen, to steer.
  • Eat the glowing dots to grow.
  • Hold the boost button to go faster for a while; boosting costs a little of your length.
  • If the head of your worm touches the body of another worm, your worm is out and turns into food.
  • If another worm runs into your body, it is out and you can eat what it leaves behind.

Tips

  • Circle around a smaller worm to trap it.
  • Use boost to cut in front of a worm, not when you are in a crowd.
  • Stay away from the red edge of the arena.

Worm styles

You can choose many worm styles, including a free Kurdistan flag worm, and you can move the joystick and boost button wherever you like in the settings.

♠️ Konkan (rummy): tips for opening and going out

By the AG Classic Games team · Updated 9 November 2026

Konkan is played at countless family gatherings and cafés in Iraq and Kurdistan. It belongs to the large family of rummy games, where players collect sets and runs. Here are some practical ideas to help you win more hands.

A quick reminder

Konkan is played with two packs of cards and jokers. On each turn you take one card – either from the closed pile or the last discarded card – then you may lay down combinations, and finally you throw one card away. A combination is three or four cards of the same rank in different suits, or a run of three or more cards in one suit. Your first lay-down, the "opening", must reach a minimum value (often 51 points). The first player to get rid of all their cards wins the hand.

1. Plan your opening early

Look at your hand as soon as you receive it and ask: which combinations am I closest to? High cards such as tens, picture cards and aces are worth more points, so they help you reach the opening value faster. On the other hand, they are expensive if you are caught holding them when another player goes out. Balance the two: keep high cards that are part of a near-complete combination, and throw away high cards that are not going anywhere.

2. Jokers are precious

A joker can replace any card, so it can complete almost any combination. Never throw a joker away, and think carefully about where to use it. Using a joker to reach your opening value is often worth it, because once you have opened you can add cards to the combinations on the table and empty your hand faster.

3. Read the discard pile

The discard pile is full of information. If the player after you keeps picking up cards from it, they are collecting something – watch which ranks and suits they take, and avoid throwing cards that help them. In the same way, the cards a player throws away tell you what they do not need.

4. Be careful what you throw

A good habit is to throw cards that have already appeared several times, or that are unlikely to complete anything for the next player. Throwing a card that the next player picks up immediately is the most common mistake in Konkan.

5. Do not wait too long

Holding cards to make a perfect hand can be tempting, but if another player goes out first, you will pay for everything in your hand – and a player who has not opened usually pays a large penalty. When your hand is risky, it is often better to open as soon as you can and reduce your cards step by step.

6. Playing in teams

With four players, partners sit opposite each other. Pay attention to the cards your partner throws and picks up, and try to leave useful cards for them. Going out is good for the whole team, so sometimes supporting your partner is the best move.

Practise

In AG Classic Games you can play Konkan against the computer to practise counting your opening points and choosing what to throw, or play online with friends in teams of two.

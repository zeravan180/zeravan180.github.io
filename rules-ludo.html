🎯 Ludo

Roll the die and race your four tokens home.

About the game

Ludo comes from the ancient Indian game pachisi. It is a simple race game for two to four players, which makes it a favourite family game in many countries.

How to play

  • Each player has four tokens in their corner base.
  • Roll the die on your turn. A six lets you bring a new token onto the start square, and usually gives you another roll.
  • Move one token forward by the number rolled around the track.
  • If you land exactly on an opponent's token, it is sent back to its base.
  • Tokens on the safe squares (often marked with a star) cannot be captured.
  • After a full lap, tokens enter your home column. The first player with all four tokens home wins.

Tips

  • Do not leave a token just in front of an opponent's token – it is easy to hit.
  • Use sixes wisely: sometimes bringing a new token out is better than moving one far ahead.
  • Keep at least one token near home so you can use small rolls well at the end.

💍 Mheibes – the ring game (Kustîrk)

A traditional Iraqi team game: one team hides a ring in a closed hand, the other team must find it.

About the game

Mheibes is a much-loved traditional game of Iraq and Kurdistan, played especially during the evenings of Ramadan. Read more in our article Mheibes: the traditional ring game of Ramadan nights.

How to play in AG Classic Games

  • Two teams play (one player each, or two players each). The team holding the ring has four hands; the ring is hidden in one of them.
  • The other team chooses a hand. “Bat” means “this hand is empty” – the hand is opened. If three empty hands are opened, the ring is in the last one and the searchers score.
  • If the searcher opens the hand that holds the ring, the hiders score. The searcher can also point at a hand and say “Here it is!”: right gives the point to the searchers, wrong gives it to the hiders.
  • The team that wins the point holds the ring next. The first team to reach the target (5, 11 or 21 points) wins.

Live studio

The game also has a full-screen studio with twelve hands, made for playing with friends during live streams. The phone hides the ring, nobody knows where it is, and the viewers say which hand to open and whether it is empty or holds the ring.

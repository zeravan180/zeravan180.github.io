🛡️ Playing games online: safety and fair play

By the AG Classic Games team · Updated 9 November 2026

Playing online makes it easy to find an opponent at any time of day. A few simple habits keep it safe and enjoyable for everyone.

Protect your account

  • Use a strong password for the account you sign in with, and turn on two-step verification if it is available.
  • Never share your password or verification codes with anyone, including people who claim to be from a game's support team.
  • Be careful with links sent in chats; they may lead to fake sign-in pages.

Keep personal information private

Your display name and profile photo are seen by other players. Avoid using your full name, phone number or address in your profile, and do not share personal details in chat. This is especially important for children and teenagers, and parents should talk with them about it.

Be respectful

Behind every opponent is a real person. Greet them, say "good game" at the end, and keep chat and voice messages friendly. Insults, threats and spam spoil the fun and can lead to an account being suspended. If someone behaves badly, use the block or report options instead of answering back.

Play fair

Cheating – using outside help, exploiting bugs or leaving a game on purpose to avoid a loss – ruins the experience for others. Good games, win or lose, are the reason we play. In AG Classic Games, online tables have a turn timer so that nobody can stall, and the game checks every move.

Take breaks

Games are most fun in reasonable amounts. Short breaks between games help you play better and keep a healthy balance with school, work and family.

If something goes wrong

If you see a bug, lose a game because of a connection problem or notice suspicious behaviour, contact the game's team with a short description and, if possible, a screenshot. For AG Classic Games, you can reach us through the contact page.

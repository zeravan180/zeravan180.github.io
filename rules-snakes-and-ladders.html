🐍 Snakes & Ladders

Climb the ladders, avoid the snakes and reach square 100.

About the game

Snakes and Ladders also began in India, where it was used to teach about good and bad deeds: ladders for virtues and snakes for mistakes. Today it is one of the first board games many children learn, because the rules are very simple and the luck of the dice keeps everyone in the game.

How to play

  • All players start before square 1.
  • On your turn, roll the die and move forward by that number.
  • If you land at the foot of a ladder, climb up to the square at its top.
  • If you land on the head of a snake, slide down to the square at its tail.
  • The first player to reach square 100 wins. In many versions you need the exact number to land on 100.

Why people love it

Because the game is almost all luck, younger and older players can compete as equals. It is also a good way for small children to practise counting.

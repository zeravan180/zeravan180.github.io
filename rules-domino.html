🔲 Dominoes

Match the ends of the tiles and be the first to empty your hand.

About the game

Dominoes is played with a set of 28 tiles. Each tile has two halves with between zero and six spots, so every pair of numbers from 0-0 to 6-6 appears exactly once. The game is loved across the Middle East, the Mediterranean and Latin America, and is often played in cafés by two players or by two teams of two.

How to play

  • Each player takes seven tiles. The remaining tiles stay face down as the boneyard (in some games there is no boneyard).
  • The first tile is laid on the table. Players then take turns adding one tile to either open end of the line.
  • A tile can be played only if one of its halves matches the number at the end where it is placed. Doubles are laid crosswise.
  • If you cannot play, you draw from the boneyard or pass, depending on the rules being used.
  • The round ends when a player plays the last tile in their hand, or when nobody can move (a blocked game).

Scoring

The winner of a round scores the total number of spots left in the opponents' hands. In a blocked game, the player or team with the fewest spots wins the round. Games are usually played to a target score, so several rounds make one match.

Tips

  • Play your heavy tiles (with many spots) early, so you lose fewer points if someone else goes out.
  • Keep a variety of numbers in your hand so you always have a move.
  • Watch which numbers your opponent cannot play – it tells you what is missing from their hand.
  • In team games, help your partner by keeping the ends open on numbers they have played before.

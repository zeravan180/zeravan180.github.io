Classic games for everyone

AG Classic Games brings together the board, card and dice games that families in Kurdistan, Iraq and the whole region have played for generations – chess, dominoes, backgammon (tawla), dama, ludo, Konkan and more – in one free app.

On this site you will find clear rules, history and tips for every game, written by us. When you are ready, open the games and play against the computer, with a friend on the same phone, or online with players from around the world.

Why we made AG Classic Games

Many of the games we grew up with were played on a café table, at family gatherings or during long winter evenings. We wanted a single place where these games can be played the way people actually play them in our region – for example dama with straight moves, Konkan with its opening points, and dominoes in teams of two.

The app is light, works on almost any phone and is available in Kurdish (Badini and Sorani), Arabic and English. There is no need to pay to play: coins used inside the games are free play currency and cannot be bought or cashed out.

Ways to play

  • Against the computer – practise any game at easy, medium or hard level, even without an internet connection.
  • With a friend on one phone – pass the phone between turns.
  • Online – find an opponent automatically, or share a five-letter table code with friends.
  • Levels and leaderboards – every finished game gives experience points, and the best players appear on the leaderboard.

Learning a new game

If you do not know a game yet, start with its guide on this site, then play a few rounds against the easy computer. Each guide explains the goal of the game, the rules step by step, and a few tips that make a big difference for new players.

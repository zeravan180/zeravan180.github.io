👨‍👩‍👧 Why board games are good for families

By the AG Classic Games team · Updated 9 November 2026

In many homes in Kurdistan and Iraq, an evening without tawla, dominoes or cards would feel incomplete. Board games are entertainment, but they also do something more: they bring people together and exercise the mind.

Time together

A game gives a family a reason to sit at the same table, away from separate screens. Grandparents can play with grandchildren, and the rules give everyone an equal chance. Many people's best childhood memories include a game with their parents or cousins.

Thinking skills

Strategy games such as chess, dama and backgammon encourage players to plan ahead, compare options and think about what the other person will do. Card games like Konkan exercise memory and counting. Even simple games train attention and concentration.

Learning to win and lose

Every game ends with a winner and a loser. For children, this is a safe way to practise both: congratulating the winner, accepting a loss calmly and trying again. Games of chance, such as snakes and ladders or ludo, are especially useful here, because everyone loses sometimes no matter how well they play.

Numbers and language

Young children learn to count by moving pieces along a track, and to add by summing dice. Reading game cards and explaining rules builds language skills. Many games played in our region are also a way to pass on words, expressions and traditions from older generations.

Patience and fair play

Games require waiting for your turn, following rules and respecting the other players. These habits carry over into everyday life. Playing online adds another lesson: being polite to people you have never met.

Keeping the mind active

For adults and older people, regular games are an enjoyable way to keep the mind busy and to stay socially connected. Many older players in cafés have played the same game with the same friends for decades.

A balanced approach

Like everything, games are best in moderation. Short sessions, breaks between games and a mix of different activities keep play healthy and fun. When games are online, it is also good for parents to know who their children are playing and chatting with.

Start with a classic

If your family wants to start, choose a game that suits everyone: snakes and ladders or ludo for young children, dominoes or tic-tac-toe for mixed ages, and chess or tawla for those who enjoy a challenge. All of them are available in AG Classic Games, with guides on this website.

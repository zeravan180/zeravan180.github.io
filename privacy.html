Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what information AG Classic Games ("we") collects through this website (zeravan180.github.io) and the AG Classic Games app, and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information – when you sign in to play online, we receive your display name, email address and profile photo from your sign-in provider (Google). Your name and photo are shown to other players.
  • Game information – your games, scores, level, free coins and the styles you choose are stored so that your progress is kept.
  • Messages and voice – chat messages and short voice clips you send at a table are delivered to the other players at that table.

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and cookies

This website uses Google AdSense to show advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also visit www.aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising. For more information, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase – sign-in and storing game data.
  • Google AdSense – advertising on this website.
  • GitHub Pages – hosting of this website.

Children

Our games are suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all related data at any time by emailing zeravan180@gmail.com from the address you use to sign in.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

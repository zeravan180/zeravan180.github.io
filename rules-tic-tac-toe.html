❌ Tic-Tac-Toe (XO)

Three in a row on a 3×3 grid – simple, fast and fun.

About the game

Tic-tac-toe, also called noughts and crosses or XO, is one of the simplest strategy games. It is played on a grid of nine squares and a game takes less than a minute. It is often the first game children use to learn about thinking ahead.

How to play

  • One player is X and the other is O. X moves first.
  • Players take turns placing their mark in an empty square.
  • The first player to get three marks in a row – across, down or diagonally – wins.
  • If all nine squares are filled and nobody has three in a row, the game is a draw.

Strategy

  • The centre square is the strongest first move because it is part of four lines.
  • Corners are the next best squares; they are each part of three lines.
  • Always check if your opponent can complete a line on the next move, and block it.
  • Try to create a fork: a move that threatens two lines at once, so your opponent cannot block both.
  • With perfect play from both sides the game always ends in a draw.

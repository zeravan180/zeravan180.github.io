♠️ Konkan (rummy)

A rummy game: build sets and runs and be the first to go out.

About the game

Konkan is a rummy game that is very popular in Iraq, Kurdistan and other parts of the Middle East. It is played with two packs of cards and jokers, by two or four players (in two teams).

How to play

  • Each player gets fourteen cards; the player who starts gets fifteen and begins by throwing one card away.
  • On your turn, take one card (from the pile or the last discarded card), lay down combinations if you can, then throw one card away.
  • A combination is either three or four cards of the same number in different suits, or three or more cards in a row in one suit.
  • A joker can stand in for any card, but every combination needs at least two real cards.
  • Your first lay-down (the opening) must be worth a minimum number of points, such as 51.
  • After opening you may add cards to any combination on the table. The first player to lay down all their cards goes out and wins the hand.

Scoring

Players who did not go out count the cards left in their hand; a player who never opened pays 100 points. Going out all at once, without opening first, doubles the points. In a longer game, the team with the fewest points after five hands wins.

Tips

  • Collect cards that can fit into more than one combination.
  • Watch what your opponents pick up from the discard pile – it shows what they are building.
  • Do not hold too many high cards late in the hand; they cost many points if someone goes out.

About AG Classic Games

AG Classic Games is a small independent project from Duhok in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq.

The project started with a single chess game for friends and grew, step by step, into a collection of the games people in our region play most: dominoes, backgammon (tawla), dama, ludo, snakes and ladders, Konkan and tic-tac-toe, together with a few original games such as AG Zar and AG Mar.

Every game is written from scratch. The rules follow the versions that are common in Kurdistan and Iraq, and we listen to players who tell us how they play at home.

What we care about

  • Simple and light – the games load fast and work on older phones.
  • In your language – Kurdish (Badini and Sorani), Arabic and English.
  • Fair play – moves are checked by the game, and online tables have a turn timer so nobody can stall.
  • Free – the coins in the games are free play currency only.
